The contact model

Scenario

Site A, the start, the route and all handling are scenario choices, not historic astronaut activities or Perseverance observations. The model shows which sources have a path of assumed contacts to Sample B. It does not show measured transfer or contamination, and it grades nothing. The solid line on the map is only the walking route.
