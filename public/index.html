      Science target · Perseverance / Mars 2020

      NASA lists Sapphire Canyon as sample 25, a 6.2 cm sedimentary core collected from Cheyava Falls in Bright Angel on Sol 1215. SHERLOC findings of organic compounds and mineral associations make sample provenance relevant to future interpretation; they do not establish life.

      Mapped coordinate: 18.49747°N, 77.30515°E, from the PDS report associated with the sample. Its coordinate datum and horizontal accuracy are not stated. B is a nominal target-area placement, not a verified drill point. The report elevation and CTX target-cell elevation differ by about 29.8 m; the cause is unresolved.

      Illustrative contact model

      Site A, start, route and human handling are scenario choices, not historic astronaut activities or Perseverance observations. The model shows possible contact-path reachability under those assumptions; it does not show measured transfer, contamination, probability, risk, safety or a crewed EVA procedure. The solid line in the map is only the walking route.

      Further mechanism context

      NASA EVA suit swabs and an Earth Mars-analog study support possible human-associated material on crew/suit surfaces and gloves; neither measures glove-to-Mars-sample transfer. MIASMMA describes planned suit-leakage measurements and dispersal modeling, not completed Mars-EVA measurements. Perseverance witness tubes monitor its robotic sample chain and do not establish an astronaut protocol.
