    Science target · Perseverance / Mars 2020

    NASA lists Sapphire Canyon as sample 25, a 6.2 cm sedimentary core collected from Cheyava Falls in Bright Angel on Sol 1215. Its sample report gives a site coordinate; location uncertainty is not provided. SHERLOC organic detections and mineral associations make sample provenance relevant to later interpretation; they do not establish life. The same PDS report coordinate maps to a valid CTX cell. The target pixel's DTM elevation differs from the dossier's listed elevation; the two should not be treated as a precise vertical match.

    Illustrative mission and contact model

    Site A and the traverse are scenario choices, not historic astronaut activities or additional Perseverance observations. The user can investigate whether an explicit contact chain connects possible human-associated material, a possible source at hypothetical A, or B's own geological origin to the sample history. Paths are model logic, not measured transfer; there are no rates, probabilities, safe-plan rankings, or crewed EVA procedure claims.

    Mechanism evidence and missing parameters

    NASA EVA suit swabs and an Earth Mars-analog study support possible human-associated material on crew/suit surfaces and gloves. Neither measures glove-to-Mars-sample transfer. MIASMMA describes planned suit-leakage measurements and dispersal modeling, not completed Mars-EVA measurements. Mars 2020 cleanliness limits apply to its robotic sample hardware and are not EVA thresholds.

    Separate robotic precedent · witness controls

    Perseverance witness tubes and drillable blanks monitor contamination in its robotic sample chain. They help interpret returned samples but do not establish a crewed-EVA glove-change or tool-use procedure.
