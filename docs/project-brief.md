# Project brief

## Constraints and goals

- Three technology students, with roughly 9–15 active human hours in total.
- Coding agents can implement quickly, but integration, data interpretation, and quality checks still use human time.
- Aim for a narrow, scientifically credible idea with a surprising point that is easy to explain.
- Target a working interactive prototype and a visually strong demo of at most 30 seconds.
- Three.js and Remotion are candidates; choose tools to fit the work.
- 3D should explain something in the data rather than serve as decoration.
- Selected direction: **Clean Approach**, a Marswalk plan that makes potential astronaut-introduced sample-contact pathways visible and keeps a witness/check record traceable. The transfer mechanism is not quantified; a route-order comparison is an illustrative scenario, not a validated mitigation.

## Earlier candidate hypotheses (not current direction)

1. How far can an astronaut walk and still return when terrain, stops, and an explicit time reserve are included?
2. How can an approach to a sampling site preserve its scientific value? Wind alone does not establish the spread of biological contamination.
3. Where can a detailed Mars map imply more certainty than its underlying data support?

These are candidates, not a requirement to implement all three. A better bounded idea may be chosen if the data support it.
